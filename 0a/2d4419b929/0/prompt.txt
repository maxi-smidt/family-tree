[P2] Commit failures do not trigger rollback. UnitOfWork.__exit__ calls commit() without a try/except, so a failed commit leaves the session dirty or in SQLAlchemy’s failed-transaction state. The route tests mask this by manually calling db.rollback() afterward. Wrap commit() and rollback before re-raising; add an assertion that the UoW itself performed the rollback.

[P2] The transaction boundary begins after part of the mutation. In create_event, add, flush, and link replacement happen before entering UnitOfWork on line 134. Similar patterns exist in stories and link-update routes. An exception during those operations bypasses UoW rollback, contradicting its “single-owner transaction boundary” contract. Move the complete mutation, including flush/link replacement, inside the context.
